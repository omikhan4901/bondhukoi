Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

BondhuKoi shows university students which of their friends are on campus, or in a place they share, without showing anyone’s location. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We wrote it to match how the app actually works; the code is public at github.com/omikhan4901/bondhukoi.

The short version

  • Your location is never stored. Your phone sends a location reading when you cross the edge of a zone (your campus or a circle’s place) or open the app. Our server uses it to answer “inside or not?”, stores only that answer, and discards the reading.
  • Only people you accept can see anything: friends, members of circles you join, and people you allow to get arrival alerts.
  • Nobody, including BondhuKoi’s admins, can see where you are or were.
  • You can pause sharing, set quiet hours, block people and delete your account at any time.

We don’t sell data, show ads, or use your data to train AI.

Who can see what

  • Friends see your name, photo, social links, and “on campus”, “away”, or the circles you share, if you share them.
  • Circle members see your name and photo, and whether you’re in that circle’s place right now (unless you turn that off for the circle).
  • People you allow to get alerts get a notification when you arrive at or leave campus (and, if you allowed it, circles you share).
  • Anyone at your university can find you by name to send a friend request. Anyone with your friend code can too.
  • BondhuKoi admins can see account details, reports and feedback to keep the service safe. They cannot see your location, status or history.

When sharing is paused, during quiet hours, or if your account is suspended, everyone sees only “Not sharing”.

Services we use

Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Mumbai, India), Google Cloud Run (the API, Mumbai), Expo (push notifications), our email provider (sign-in codes only), Google Maps (the map when you draw a circle’s place) and Cloudflare (this website and the admin console). They process data only to provide their service to us.

Your choices and rights

  • Pause sharing, turn off campus sharing, set quiet hours, turn off detection per circle, or choose 24-hour history: You → Sharing and quiet hours.
  • See everyone who can see you and stop any of them: You → Who can see me.
  • Change or remove your details: You → Name and social links.
  • Delete your account and everything linked to it: You → Delete account, or without the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or ask a question: [contact email].

Age

BondhuKoi is for university students. You must be at least 16 to use it.

Security

Connections are encrypted, the database refuses direct access from the app, and admins must use two-factor sign-in. Our security review is public in the code repository. Found a problem? Tell us privately.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we’ll update this page and tell you in the app before it takes effect.
