How it works

1

Sign up with your university email

Only students of your university can join. A code in your inbox confirms it’s you.

2

Add friends by code or QR

They say yes before anyone sees anything. Remove or block anyone, any time.

3

Make circles with a place

Draw your thesis lab or the library on the map. Members see who’s there right now.

Your privacy, in plain words

Your location is never stored

Your phone sends a reading only when you cross a zone’s edge. The server answers “on campus?”, keeps that answer, and throws the reading away.

Not even we can see where you are

The admin console has no way to show anyone’s location or history. That’s enforced in the code and tested.

You’re in control

Pause sharing in one tap. Quiet from 6 PM to 6 AM by default. “Who can see me” lists everyone who can see anything.

The code is open. CSE students: read it, and if you find a hole, tell us privately.
