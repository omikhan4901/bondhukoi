Delete your BondhuKoi account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BondhuKoi and go to You.
  2. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email omi@omikhan.com from your university email address with the subject “Delete my BondhuKoi account”. We’ll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. We only act on requests from the account’s own email address.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo, social links and friend code
  • Your friendships, requests, arrival-alert permissions and blocks
  • Your status and your arrivals and departures history
  • Your circle memberships (circles you ran are handed to another member, or deleted if you were the last one)
  • Your push tokens and your sign-in account

Reports you sent are kept without your name for up to 1 year, to keep other students safe. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.
