## English posts

### 1. Founder story (YOUR profile, launch day)
**Graphic:** `01-launch.png`

> Every day between classes I'd send the same message to five group chats: "Who's on
> campus?" 😅
>
> Location-sharing apps could answer that, but I didn't want my friends (or some company)
> to see a dot of exactly where I am all day. So I built **BondhuKoi**.
>
> It only ever says "On campus" or "Away". Your phone checks when you cross the campus
> edge, the server answers "inside or not", and the location itself is thrown away. Not
> even I, running the servers, can see where anyone is. The code is open if you want to
> check.
>
> You can also make circles for your thesis lab or club room and see who's there right now.
> Quiet from 6 PM to 6 AM by default.
>
> It's free, Android for now, and open to NSU students first: [link]
> Tell me what you think, honestly. 🙏

### 4. Circles
**Graphic:** `03-circles.png` · **Clip:** `videos/circle-square.mp4`

> Your thesis group has a lab. Your friends have "their" table in the library.
>
> Make a circle, draw the place on the map (tap the corners, tap the first one to close),
> and the circle shows who's there right now. The map of the place becomes the circle's
> picture.
>
> Members only see whether you're inside that place. Turn it off per circle any time.

### 5. Privacy explainer (carousel, 5 images)
**Graphics:** `04-privacy-1.png` … `04-privacy-5.png`

> How BondhuKoi works without knowing where you are 👇
> 1. Your phone watches the edge of your campus, not your location.
> 2. When you cross it, one reading goes to our server.
> 3. The server answers "inside or not?" and throws the reading away.
> 4. Friends you accepted see only the answer.
> 5. Pause, quiet hours, and "Who can see me" are one tap away.
>
> The code is public: github.com/omikhan4901/bondhukoi

### 6. Quiet hours
**Graphic:** `05-quiet.png`

> From 6 PM to 6 AM, BondhuKoi shares nothing. Friends just see "Not sharing", the same as
> if you'd paused, so nobody can tell the difference.
>
> Change the hours, or turn it off, in You → Sharing and quiet hours.

### 7. Who can see me
**Graphic:** `06-who-can-see-me.png`

> One screen in BondhuKoi lists everyone who can see anything about you: friends, circles,
> and anyone you allowed to get arrival alerts. Each with a button to stop it.
>
> If an app knows who can see you, you should too.
