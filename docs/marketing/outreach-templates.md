# Outreach and reply templates

Copy, personalise, send. Short and personal beats long and formal. Never send the same
message to hundreds of people at once.

---

## Group admins: asking to post

> Hi [admin name]! I'm [your name], [year] [department] at [university]. I built a free
> app for students called BondhuKoi: it shows which friends are on campus without sharing
> anyone's location (it's open source, so people can check).
>
> Could I share one post about it in [group name]? Happy to follow your rules. Thank you! 🙏

## Club presidents: a circle for your club

> Hi [name]! I'm [your name] from [department]. I built BondhuKoi, an app that shows which
> friends are on campus, privately (only "on campus" or "away", never a map).
>
> It has "circles": a group with a place. [Club name] could have a circle for your club room
> or event venue, so members see who's there right now.
>
> Could I show it in 5 minutes at your next meeting? I'll set up the circle with you. 😊

## Replies to common comments

**"Isn't this a stalking app?"**
> Fair question, and it's why it's built the way it is: nobody sees where you are, only
> "on campus" or "away"; nobody sees anything until you accept them; alerts need your
> yes; you can pause, block, or see everyone who can see you. And the code is open to
> check. If anyone misuses it, report them in the app and we act on it.

**"Why does it need location 'all the time'?"**
> So your status changes when you arrive, without opening the app. The phone only wakes
> BondhuKoi when you cross your campus or circle edge, so it barely uses battery. You can
> choose "only while using" instead; status just updates when you open it.

**"iPhone?"**
> Android first. If enough people ask, iPhone next. Comment and I'll count you in!

**"My university isn't there."**
> We open one campus at a time with a student ambassador. Want to be ours at [university]?

**"It didn't update when I got to campus."**
> Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Realme, Vivo) stop apps in the background. You → Help has the
> fix; it takes a minute. Tell me your phone model if it still doesn't work 🙏
