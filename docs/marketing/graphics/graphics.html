Your phone watches the edge of your campus.

Not where you are: just whether you cross the line.

BondhuKoi
2

When you cross it, one reading goes to our server.

That happens only at the edge, or when you open the app.

BondhuKoi
3

The server answers “inside or not?” and throws the reading away.

Your location is never stored. Not even the admins can see it.

Quiet hours

Quiet from 6 PM to 6 AM.

Nothing is shared at night. Friends just see “Not sharing”, the same as when you pause, so nobody can tell which.
