# Google Play store listing

**App name (30 max):** BondhuKoi: friends on campus

**Short description (80 max):**
> See which friends are on campus, without anyone seeing where you are.

**Full description (4,000 max):**
> BondhuKoi (বন্ধু কই?) shows university students which of their friends are on campus right
> now, without sharing anyone's location.
>
> ONLY "ON CAMPUS" OR "AWAY"
> Friends see a simple status, never a dot on a map. Your phone checks when you cross your
> campus edge; the server answers "inside or not?", keeps only that answer, and throws the
> location away.
>
> CIRCLES FOR THE PLACES YOU MEET
> Make a circle for your thesis lab, the library or your club room. Draw the place on the
> map and see who's there right now. Members only see whether you're inside that place.
>
> YOU'RE IN CONTROL
> • Nobody sees anything until you accept them
> • Pause sharing in one tap
> • Quiet from 6 PM to 6 AM by default
> • Turn off any circle, or campus sharing
> • "Who can see me" lists everyone who can see anything about you
> • Block and report
>
> ARRIVAL ALERTS, WITH PERMISSION
> A friend can ask to be told when you get to campus. You say yes or no, and either of you
> can stop it any time.
>
> STUDENTS ONLY
> Sign up with your university email. Only students of your university can find you by name.
>
> NOT EVEN WE CAN SEE WHERE YOU ARE
> BondhuKoi's admins have no way to see anyone's location or history. The code is open
> source: github.com/omikhan4901/bondhukoi
>
> BondhuKoi uses location in the background only to detect when you enter or leave your
> campus or circles' places. You can choose "only while using the app" instead.
>
> Now open at North South University. More universities soon.
